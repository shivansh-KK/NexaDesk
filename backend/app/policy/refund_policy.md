# ShopAssist Electronics – Return & Refund Policy

## 1. Overview
At ShopAssist Electronics, customer satisfaction is our top priority. We offer a structured return and refund process for products purchased directly through our online store. All monetary values are processed in UAE Dirhams (AED).

## 2. Refund Eligibility Criteria
A refund request will be accepted and processed only when all the following conditions are met:
1. **Order Status**: The order must have the status **"delivered"**. Orders in "processing", "shipped", "cancelled", or already "returned" status are ineligible for returns or refunds.
2. **Return Window**: The return request must be submitted within **14 calendar days** from the verified delivery date (`delivery_date`).
3. **Item Condition**: 
   - Physical hardware (smartphones, laptops, accessories, audio gear) can be returned within the 14-day window.
   - **Opened Software is Non-Refundable**: Software licenses or physical software packages whose seals are broken or digital keys delivered/opened cannot be refunded.
   - **Gift Cards are Non-Refundable**: Digital or physical store gift cards are strictly final sale.
4. **Frequency Limit**: Maximum of **one (1) refund request per order**. Subsequent duplicate requests for the same order ID will be rejected.

## 3. High-Value Orders & Escalation
- For orders with a total refundable value exceeding **5,000 AED**, automated refund creation is disabled.
- Any refund request above **5,000 AED** must be escalated to a human supervisor for manual review and identity verification before processing.

## 4. Refund Processing & Method
- Approved refunds will be credited back to the original payment method within 5–7 business days.
- In-store credit options are available upon customer request.

## 5. Shipping & Delivery Policy
- Standard delivery takes 2–4 business days within the UAE.
- Express same-day delivery is available for eligible electronics in Dubai and Abu Dhabi.
- Tracking numbers and real-time statuses ("processing", "shipped", "delivered") are provided for all registered orders.

## 6. Support & Escalations
- Customers experiencing hardware defects under warranty may request support tickets or escalation.
- Abusive language, repeated unverified requests, or requests requiring supervisor authority will be immediately escalated to human agents.
